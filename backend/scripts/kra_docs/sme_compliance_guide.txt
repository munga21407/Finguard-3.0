KENYA REVENUE AUTHORITY — SME COMPLIANCE GUIDE
Tax Obligations for Small and Medium Enterprises in Kenya | Updated 2024

SECTION 1: TAX REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------
Every business operating in Kenya must register with KRA upon commencement of
business. The following registrations may be required:

1.1 PIN Registration
  All businesses must obtain a Personal Identification Number (PIN) from KRA.
  Required for: opening bank accounts, importing goods, tendering for contracts,
  registering property, and applying for government licences.

1.2 VAT Registration
  Mandatory when annual turnover exceeds KES 5,000,000.
  Voluntary registration permitted below threshold.
  Apply via iTax portal within 30 days of exceeding the threshold.

1.3 PAYE Registration
  Required as soon as the business employs one or more employees.
  Register on iTax and obtain an employer reference number.

1.4 Turnover Tax Registration
  Available to businesses with annual turnover KES 1,000,000 – 50,000,000.
  Replaces Corporate Income Tax; rate is 3% of gross monthly turnover.

SECTION 2: MONTHLY COMPLIANCE CALENDAR
----------------------------------------
Day 9 of each month:
  - PAYE (employee income tax) remittance

Day 20 of each month:
  - VAT return filing and payment
  - Withholding Tax remittance
  - Turnover Tax (TOT) payment
  - Excise Duty (if applicable)

Day 9 of the month following each quarter:
  - Agency Revenue (if applicable)

6 months after accounting year-end:
  - Annual corporate income tax return (IT2C)
  - Instalment tax reconciliation

SECTION 3: ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) OBLIGATIONS
-----------------------------------------------------
Under the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (POCAMLA), businesses
in designated non-financial businesses and professions (DNFBPs) must:

3.1 Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
  - Verify customer identity before establishing a business relationship
  - Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers and politically exposed persons (PEPs)
  - Ongoing monitoring of business relationships

3.2 Suspicious Transaction Reporting
  Large Cash Transactions: Any single transaction exceeding KES 1,000,000 in cash
  must be reported to the Financial Reporting Centre (FRC) within 3 business days.

  Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs): File with the FRC within 3 days of suspicion.

  M-Pesa and mobile money transactions exceeding KES 500,000 in a single day
  from a single counterparty should trigger enhanced due diligence.

3.3 Record Keeping
  All CDD records must be retained for at least 7 years from the date of the
  last transaction.

SECTION 4: DIGITAL SERVICES AND E-COMMERCE TAX
------------------------------------------------
With effect from 1 January 2021 (subsequently strengthened in 2024):

4.1 Digital Service Tax (DST)
  Non-resident digital marketplace operators providing digital services to Kenyan
  consumers must register for DST at 1.5% of the gross transaction value.

4.2 Significant Economic Presence (SEP) Tax
  Non-resident entities with significant digital presence in Kenya (threshold:
  annual revenue from Kenya ≥ KES 5,000,000) are subject to a 30% tax on
  deemed profit (calculated as 10% of gross Kenya revenue).

4.3 Local E-Commerce Platforms
  Kenyan-registered e-commerce businesses are subject to standard VAT (16%) and
  CIT (30%) on profits. eTIMS integration is mandatory for VAT-registered operators.

SECTION 5: PENALTIES AND INTEREST SUMMARY
-------------------------------------------
Late Filing Penalties:
  - VAT return: KES 10,000 or 5% of tax payable (whichever is greater)
  - Income tax return: 5% per month, capped at 25% of tax due
  - PAYE return: KES 10,000 or 25% of tax due (whichever is greater)

Late Payment Interest:
  - All taxes: 1% per month, simple interest, on outstanding tax
  - Instalment tax shortfall: 20% of the shortfall amount

Civil Penalties:
  - Tax evasion: 100% of evaded tax
  - Failure to maintain records: KES 500,000
  - Failure to submit information to KRA: KES 200,000 per instance

Criminal Sanctions:
  - Tax fraud: 3 years imprisonment and/or fine equal to 200% of evaded tax
  - Obstruction of KRA officers: 6 months imprisonment

SECTION 6: TAX INCENTIVES FOR SMES
-------------------------------------
6.1 Export Processing Zone (EPZ) Companies
  - 10-year tax holiday (0% CIT)
  - 25% CIT rate thereafter for 10 additional years

6.2 Special Economic Zone (SEZ) Companies
  - 10% CIT rate for first 10 years
  - 15% CIT rate thereafter

6.3 Start-up Tax Incentives (Finance Act 2023)
  - 5% CIT rate for the first 3 years for companies incorporated after January 2023
  - 100% capital allowance for manufacturing equipment in the first year

6.4 Agricultural Sector Incentives
  - 0% CIT for the first 10 years for new agri-processing businesses
  - Deductible environmental levy for sustainable farming practices

SECTION 7: iTAX PORTAL OBLIGATIONS
-------------------------------------
All KRA interactions must be conducted through the iTax portal (itax.kra.go.ke):
  - Tax registration and deregistration
  - Return filing (monthly VAT, PAYE, annual CIT)
  - Tax payment (integrated with RTGS, mobile banking, card payments)
  - Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) application
  - Transfer pricing documentation submission
  - Objection and appeal filing

Businesses that fail to use iTax for mandatory transactions may face penalties
and will be ineligible for the Tax Compliance Certificate needed for:
  - Government tenders
  - Import and export licences
  - Obtaining credit from banks
  - Renewing business permits
